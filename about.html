My Professional Journey

Hello, I'm Nneka Okereke

I'm a Project Manager focused on workforce development, multi-site delivery, and stakeholder engagement.

At Octopus Energy I was promoted in 2026 and selected from 100+ applicants across all sites to help design a professional development programme for colleagues working towards Pro and Master standard in their chosen field. I plan the programme in Asana, develop the learning content in Genially, and I will facilitate the courses for colleagues who apply to progress. Alongside that, I have coordinated 250+ solar and battery projects (150 installations completed), completed 350+ EV charger installations, and overseen 20+ heat pump programmes, with a 98% on-time delivery rate and 90% positive customer feedback.

On live installations I use RAID tracking, CRM-led communication and stage checklists so engineers, suppliers and customers stay aligned, compliant and clear on what happens next.
